why_text: "This project was developed for a teacher at the Misericordia Home, supporting adults with intellectual and developmental disabilities. Students in the classroom had diverse sensory needs that traditional educational tools struggled to accommodate, making it challenging to teach concepts like American currency safely. The Money Board addresses this by integrating real coins and facsimile dollar bills that can be handled without risk of removal or choking. Additional sensory features– such as acrylic dots corresponding to bill values and raised clear acrylic over the large denomination numbers– enhance tactile learning and reinforce numerical recognition."
