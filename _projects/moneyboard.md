why_text: "This project was made for a teacher at the Misericordia home who taught adults with intellectual and developmental disabilities. The gentlemen in her classroom had many sensory needs that were difficult to meet with traditional educational devices and objects, and made teaching the concept of American currency difficult. More specifically, it would have been hard to show them real coins and bills without worrying about them becoming a choking hazard. The Money Board solves this problem by including real coins and facimile dollar bills that look like the real thing and can be physically touched but not removed from the board. The dollar bill side includes extra sensory features such as acrylic dots corresponding to the bills' values and extra clear acrylic over the big numbers on the back."
